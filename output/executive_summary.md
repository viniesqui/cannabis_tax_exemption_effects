Because standard financial-data APIs report only total income tax expense and never isolate the Section 280E charge, I engineered a proprietary Python pipeline that programmatically pulls the AdvisorShares Pure US Cannabis ETF (MSOS) holdings file, aggregates total-return-swap and direct-equity exposure by issuer, resolves each operator to its SEC CIK, retrieves its most recent audited annual report (10-K/40-F) from EDGAR, isolates the Income Taxes footnote from the Inline XBRL, and uses a schema-constrained LLM to transcribe the statutory-to-effective tax rate reconciliation line by line, while deterministic code selects the 280E line, verifies that the reconciliation ties to reported tax expense and cross-checks net income against the issuer's XBRL facts. Across the five largest SEC-reporting operators in the fund (Trulieve Cannabis Corp. (TRLV), Curaleaf Holdings, Inc. (CURLF), Green Thumb Industries Inc. (GTBIF), Verano Holdings Corp. (VRNO) and Glass House Brands Inc. (GLAS); together 87.0% of MSOS net assets, swap and direct exposure combined), the FY2025 footnotes disclose an aggregate Section 280E penalty of $394.8M, equivalent to 8.5% of their combined $4.63B of revenue (for Trulieve Cannabis Corp., Curaleaf Holdings, Inc., Verano Holdings Corp. and Glass House Brands Inc., which file as if 280E does not apply, the figure is the change in the uncertain-tax-position reserve their footnotes attribute to 280E, which can include interest, penalties and prior-year positions; Green Thumb Industries Inc. reserves for 280E without attributing an amount to it and is carried at zero). Section 280E denies every deduction other than cost of goods sold to a business trafficking in a Schedule I or II substance, so these operators are taxed on gross profit rather than operating income; rescheduling to Schedule III ends that disallowance and restores the deductibility of SG&A. Because the 280E charge in the reconciliation is the tax on those disallowed deductions, eliminating it reduces income tax expense dollar for dollar with no change to revenue, gross margin or operating costs, so the penalty drops straight to the bottom line: on a pro forma basis the cohort's aggregate net loss narrows from $521.2M to $126.4M, a 76% reduction, a $394.8M improvement in aggregate profitability, with the number of profitable operators rising from 1 to 2 of 5.
